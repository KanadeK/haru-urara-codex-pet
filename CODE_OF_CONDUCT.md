# Code of conduct

Be kind, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualization of the character, threats, doxxing,
or hostile pile-ons are not welcome. Discuss the work, not the worth of the
person doing it. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict
participation when needed to keep the project safe.

If a problem should not be handled in public, contact the maintainer through
GitHub's private reporting channel.
