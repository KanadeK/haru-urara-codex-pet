- Anchor both feet and the lower body to one stable baseline in every direction.
- Let the eyes lead each look, followed by a small head turn and then the horse ears.
- Keep shoulder and upper-body movement restrained; never rotate the whole sprite.
- Preserve the same face width, eye spacing, hair volume, ear length, and full-body scale.
- Let the mane, tail, and green ear ribbons follow through only slightly and remain attached.
- Keep both hands close to the neutral torso pose so the look loop reads as attention, not a new action.
- Preserve the deep-brown outline and the pink, white, green, and yellow palette in all directions.
